Live Labels privacy policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Live Labels is a Chrome extension that prints a label when an item sells in a seller's live show. This policy explains what the extension reads, what it keeps, and what leaves your computer.

What the extension reads

  • Your live show page on whatnot.com and tiktok.com (and eBay Live, Palmstreet, Poshmark or any other selling site, only if you switch it on and allow access). The extension reads the sale announcements on that page: the buyer's username, the item name and the price. It uses them only to print your label and list the sale in the side panel. It doesn't read other websites. It changes the page only when you run one of your own show controls: it then presses the button you named or types your message into the show chat, on your behalf, the same as if you did it yourself.
  • Downloaded shipping labels, only if you turn on shipping-label printing: the extension notices when you download a shipping-label PDF so it can print it.

What stays on your computer

These are stored in your browser (Chrome's extension storage) and never sent to us:

  • your settings and label design, and your logo;
  • the list of recent sales (up to 300), with buyer usernames, items and prices, so you can reprint labels. You can clear it at any time under All settings → Clear sales history, and it's removed when you uninstall the extension;
  • your license key, and the date your free trial started. The trial date is also saved in Chrome sync, if you use it, so reinstalling doesn't restart the trial.

What is sent, and to whom

  • Polar (polar.sh), our payment provider. When you activate your subscription, the extension sends your license key to Polar to check that the subscription is active, and checks it again about twice a day. Nothing about your sales is sent. When you subscribe, you pay on Polar's checkout page; Polar's own privacy policy covers your payment and account details. We never see your card details.
  • PrintNode (printnode.com), only if you choose it as your printing method. Your labels (the label image with the buyer, item, price and your logo) and your PrintNode API key are sent to PrintNode so it can print them on your printer.
  • Your printer. Labels are sent to your printer directly (USB, Bluetooth or serial), through Chrome's print dialog, or through QZ Tray, a program that runs on your own computer.
  • "Save page snapshot" (in the side panel's help section) saves a file to your computer with the text of your live show page, to help set up sale detection. It is only created when you click the button, and only shared if you send it to someone yourself.

That's all. The extension has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't sell, rent or share your data, and we don't use it for anything other than printing your labels.

Your choices

  • Turn auto-print off at any time in the side panel.
  • Clear your sales history in the settings.
  • Remove your license key under Your plan → Remove key.
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored in your browser.

Children

Live Labels is a tool for sellers and isn't directed at children under 13.
