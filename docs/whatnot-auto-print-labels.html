How to auto-print labels during a Whatnot live show

By Amir Rostami, a Whatnot and eBay seller and the maker of Live Labels. Updated November 22, 2026.

Live Labels is a Chrome extension that prints a label the moment an auction ends or an item sells in your Whatnot show, with the lot number, the buyer's username, the item, the price, the buyer's box number and your shop logo. You stick the label on the item and keep selling.

How to set it up

  1. Add Live Labels to Chrome and run the setup wizard. It connects your thermal label printer and prints a test label in about two minutes.
  2. Open your Whatnot show in Chrome on your computer (you can still stream from your phone).
  3. Open the Live Labels side panel and turn on Auto-print.
  4. Sell. Each time an auction ends or an item sells, a label comes out of your printer.

What prints on each Whatnot label

  • Lot number that counts up on its own
  • Buyer username and their box number: every item one buyer wins gets the same box number
  • Item name and price, exactly as shown in the sale announcement
  • Your shop logo, in black and white for thermal printers

Why sellers print labels during the show

Without a label, someone has to write down who bought what, or match the sales list to a pile of items after the show. With a label on each item as it sells, every buyer's items are already marked with their box number when the show ends, so packing is a matter of grouping by box.

Printers that work

USB and Bluetooth thermal label printers print directly from Chrome, and any printer installed on your computer works through Chrome's print dialog. Built-in settings cover KNAON, JADENS, Nelko, Rongta, Phomemo, MUNBYN, POLONO, iDPRT, Rollo, Arkscan, Zebra, DYMO and Brother QL. Label sizes run from 2" × 1" up to 4" × 6".

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to run the Whatnot show from my computer?

No. Many sellers stream from a phone. Keep your show open in Chrome on a computer next to you with the Live Labels side panel on, and it prints as items sell.

Does it print Whatnot shipping labels?

It prints a sale label for each item during the show. Shipping labels still come from Whatnot after the show; Live Labels can optionally auto-print the shipping label PDFs you download.

Can I reprint a label?

Yes. Every sale is listed in the side panel with one-click reprint, and you can print a label by hand for sales made off-camera.

Can I start auctions or giveaways from a hotkey?

Yes. Show controls (beta) press a button on your show page, such as Start auction or Giveaway, or send a message to the chat from a keyboard shortcut; macro pads and Stream Decks work by sending the same shortcut.
