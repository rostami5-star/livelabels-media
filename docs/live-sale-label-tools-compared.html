Printing live-sale labels: by hand, desktop app or Chrome extension

By Amir Rostami, a Whatnot and eBay seller and the maker of Live Labels. Updated November 22, 2026.

Live sellers label sold items in one of three ways: writing them by hand, running a desktop app that watches the show, or using a Chrome extension that reads the show page in the browser you already use. Live Labels is the Chrome extension option.

1. Writing labels by hand

No cost and no setup, but someone has to write the buyer and item for every sale while the show runs, and handwriting slows down fast shows.

2. A desktop app

Installed software that watches your show and sends labels to a printer. It runs outside the browser and has to be installed and kept running on your computer.

3. A Chrome extension (Live Labels)

Runs inside Chrome next to your show page. Nothing else to install: add it from the Chrome Web Store, connect your printer with the setup wizard, and turn on Auto-print in the side panel. Your sales history, logo and settings stay in your browser.

What to check before choosing any tool

  • Which platforms it reads (Whatnot, TikTok Shop, eBay Live, Poshmark) and which sale types (auctions, Buy It Now, giveaways)
  • Whether it works with the printer you already own
  • What is printed: buyer, item, price, box number, your logo
  • The price after any trial, and whether labels are unlimited
  • What data it collects

Frequently asked questions

Which platforms does Live Labels support?

Whatnot and TikTok Shop live shows, eBay Live auctions and Poshmark Posh Show auctions, plus Palmstreet (beta) and other live-selling sites you add in its settings.
