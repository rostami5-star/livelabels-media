Live Labels Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Plans and automatic renewal

  • Free trial: 14 days with every feature. No card is needed, and nothing is charged when it ends.
  • Monthly: $10 per month, plus any tax shown at checkout. Renews automatically every month until you cancel.
  • Yearly: $99 per year, plus any tax shown at checkout. Renews automatically every year until you cancel.

Polar Software, Inc. (polar.sh) processes your payment as our merchant of record. Your receipt email from Polar repeats your price, how often it renews and how to cancel.

How to cancel

You can cancel online at any time, the same way you subscribed. No email or phone call is needed.

  1. Open the link to your Polar customer portal in any Live Labels receipt email, or go to polar.sh/element7947/portal and sign in with the code Polar emails you.
  2. Choose your Live Labels subscription.
  3. Click Cancel subscription.

Cancelling stops all future charges. Live Labels keeps working until the end of the month or year you already paid for. If you have trouble, email info@element7947.com and we'll cancel it for you within one business day.

Refunds

  • Monthly charges: not refunded, because you can cancel before the next one. Cancelling stops the next charge.
  • Yearly purchases and yearly renewals: full refund if you ask within 14 days of the charge.
  • Our mistake: if you were charged twice, charged after cancelling, or Live Labels didn't work for reasons on our side and we couldn't fix it, we'll refund the affected charge.
  • Polar: Polar may also issue a refund on its own within 60 days of a purchase.

To ask for a refund, email info@element7947.com from the address you paid with. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Polar. How long it takes to appear depends on your card issuer.

Reminders and price changes

  • Before each yearly renewal, we email you a reminder with the date, the amount and how to cancel.
  • If the price changes, we email you at least 30 days before it applies to you, and you can cancel before then.

Your legal rights

This policy doesn't limit any rights you have under the law where you live, including any right to cancel or withdraw from a purchase.
