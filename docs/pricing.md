# Pricing – Live Labels

Last updated: October 30, 2026. All prices in USD.

## Free trial
- Price: $0 for 14 days
- Limits: none; every feature, unlimited labels
- No credit card needed to start

## Monthly
- Price: $10/month
- Limits: unlimited labels, one computer per license key
- Cancel any time

## Yearly
- Price: $99/year (two months free compared with monthly)
- Limits: unlimited labels, one computer per license key

Test prints are always free. Payments are handled by Polar (polar.sh), the merchant of record. Founding members keep their price for as long as they stay subscribed.
